# Privacy Policy for Diffy

**Last updated: August 30, 2026**

Thank you for using **Diffy** ("Visual JSON Diff"). Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding data collection and usage.

---

### 1. Information Collection and Use

**Diffy does NOT collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information, browsing history, or user data.**

- **Local Storage Only**: All JSON data, graph nodes, and settings created within Diffy are stored strictly locally in your browser using Chrome's `chrome.storage.local` API.
- **No Remote Servers**: Diffy operates completely offline in your browser. It does not send any data to external servers, APIs, or third-party tracking services.
- **No Analytics / Telemetry**: We do not use cookies, web beacons, analytics SDKs, or tracking mechanisms.

---

### 2. Permissions Justification

- **`storage` / `unlimitedStorage`**: Used solely to persist your JSON nodes, flow graph layouts, and editor preferences locally on your machine so your work is saved between browser sessions.

---

### 3. Third-Party Services

Diffy does not integrate with or transmit data to any third-party services or advertising networks.

---

### 4. Children's Privacy

Diffy does not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children.

---

### 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected with a revised "Last updated" date at the top of this document.

---

### 6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about this Privacy Policy, please open an issue on GitHub or reach out to the developer.

---

# 隐私权政策 (Diffy)

**最近更新日期：2026年8月30日**

感谢您使用 **Diffy**（可视化 JSON Diff 工具）。我们非常重视您的隐私。本隐私权政策旨在向您说明我们对数据的处理方式。

### 1. 数据收集与使用

**Diffy 不会收集、传输、出售或共享您的任何个人数据、浏览记录或敏感信息。**

- **纯本地存储**：您在 Diffy 中输入和处理的所有 JSON 数据、图表节点及配置均仅保存在您本地浏览器的 `chrome.storage.local` 中。
- **无远程数据传输**：Diffy 完全在您的本地浏览器端运行，不会向任何外部服务器或第三方服务发送数据。
- **无数据分析与统计**：我们不包含任何 Cookie、数据分析代码（Analytics）或追踪器。

### 2. 权限使用说明

- **`storage` / `unlimitedStorage`**：仅用于在本地保存您的 JSON 节点、连线关系及偏好设置，以便您下次打开时恢复工作区。

### 3. 联系方式

如果您对本隐私权政策有任何疑问，欢迎在 GitHub 仓库中提交 Issue 联系开发者。
